How a scan becomes an explanation

The pipeline behind every result

01

Profile

Age, conditions, and preferred language, stored once.

02

Scan & recognize

OCR reads the strip or prescription and matches it to a medicine.

03

Personalize

Uses, precautions, and risk are checked against your conditions.

04

Translate & speak

The explanation is rewritten in your language and read aloud.

Safety boundary — MedGuide AI is a hackathon prototype for medication information and accessibility. It does not diagnose or prescribe. Always follow your physician's instructions.
